# Source Universe, Legal / Privacy / GRC News Briefing

*A frequency-ranked starter set of 600+ public sources for a daily legal/privacy/GRC news-curation routine: law-firm insight pages, privacy/compliance publications, and regulators. Aggregated from one practitioner's real-world reading list. Public sources only; no proprietary or organizational content. Use as a seed, add and prune for your own jurisdiction and practice.*

## How to read this
- Number in parentheses = how often the source appeared in the reading set (a rough signal of how reliably it produces relevant items).
- The top ~50 produce most of the high-signal output. Start there.
- Skews US + UK/EU legal (AI regulation, privacy, cybersecurity, data protection, GRC).
